# Movie Cars — screen imagery and artist references

Updated 18 October 2026. This supersedes the Movie Cars portion of
`studio-movie-cars-beta.md`.

## Screen imagery

The gallery shows actual published movie/TV imagery, not generic car renders
or AI-generated film stills. The image files remain on their publishers' hosts.

The studio still and clip previews retain their respective copyrights; they
are **not** covered by the 3D models' Creative Commons licenses and are not
represented as copyright-free or as evidence of a studio partnership. Source
links and rights-holder notices appear alongside the imagery. Publication must
respect the applicable publisher terms and any permissions required for its
intended context; attribution alone is not a license grant.

The Universal image uses its publisher's size/quality parameters. YouTube
previews are displayed directly, without a CapCar image proxy. Card previews are
smaller than the selected hero. An unavailable high-resolution YouTube preview
falls back to the same video's normal preview—not a stock car image. If both
fail, a neutral placeholder and the original-source link remain.

## Separate 3D workspace

The workspace is closed initially. Opening it still does not load a 3D model
until the visitor chooses the viewer's load action. Changing cars unmounts the
previous workspace/viewer. No video iframe, autoplay, Sketchfab SDK or GLB is
requested by the initial gallery.
