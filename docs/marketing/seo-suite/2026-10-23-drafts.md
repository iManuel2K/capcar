## Maintenance-record brief: roles 031, 036, 051-053

Improve `/en/journal/bmw-diy-wartung-dokumentieren` rather than opening a second
same-intent article. Audience: an owner who wants a usable record after a real
maintenance job. Objective: know what to save, understand owner-record limits,
and continue in the existing tracker/checklist flow.

Required original evidence:

1. A real completed job and actual vehicle generation/year/engine context,
   confirmed by the owner; a 318i badge alone is insufficient.
2. Actual date, mileage, confirmed part reference, paid amount, what was checked
   after completion and any unresolved observation. Redact unrelated receipt data.
3. An authentic screenshot of the resulting record, with consent and private
   identifiers removed. A wireframe or generated picture is not owner evidence.
4. A clear distinction between owner-performed and workshop work and the exact
   applicable instruction/source. No expert credential or safety sign-off is invented.

Suggested article sequence: what a useful record answers; one real redacted
entry; field-by-field explanation; what the record does not certify; unresolved
issues and when to seek qualified help; continue with the maintenance tracker.
Keep existing illustrative examples labeled until actual evidence replaces them.

Success check: a reader can identify the vehicle, work, cost and remaining issue,
then reach the relevant tracker/checklist. Activation impact requires analytics
and an authorized account-flow test; editorial clarity alone does not prove it.
