# CapCar: twelve practical campaign posts

Prepared **22 October 2026** for the 23 October–21 November campaign. These are scripts and recording briefs, not finished media or published posts. The original source material is the founder's own recording of CapCar's actual UI, their own notes and any car footage they have permission to use. No customer stories or testimonials are assumed.

## Production and reuse rules

Use the [growth plan's daily schedule](growth-plan.md) so filming, editing, uploading and replies stay inside 45 minutes. Eight simple video masters and four text assets are enough. One phone, window light and the phone's existing editor are sufficient; do not buy software, music or props. Read scripts naturally rather than performing a polished advert. Keep the clips around 20–35 seconds if the spoken pace permits; cut an optional shot rather than speed through a caveat.

Instagram Reels is the default home where the founder has an existing account. The calendar also assigns primary TikTok and YouTube Shorts versions so each platform has a deliberate use. If an account does not exist or cannot be used, publish the same master on an existing channel and retain the other version as a draft; creating accounts is outside this task. Threads or Bluesky text versions use an existing account. No X-specific campaign is proposed.

Create a clean vertical master, readable captions and a first-frame headline. Reuse the original export, not a downloaded watermarked post. Keep original voice audio; add platform-specific music only if its permitted use has been checked, and never export platform-licensed music into another service. No algorithm, reach or “best posting time” claims are needed. A single normal caption and, if useful, a couple of topical hashtags are enough.

**Shared product facts:** saving a personal car/plan needs an account; guest draft entry is browser-local; beta is free, not promised free forever. A saved job is planned until actually completed. Public BMW costs and visuals are illustrative. Parts research does not guarantee fitment. [Homepage](https://capcar.dev/), [platform](https://capcar.dev/platform), [onboarding documentation](../garage-onboarding-and-build-story.md).

Use only the public example or an existing account's record the founder wants to show. Do not create a production account for content. Redact plates, VINs, names, email, addresses, private receipts and tokens. Review the exported clip frame by frame for accidental private tabs or notifications. Keep “Illustrative example” visible on public example captures. Demo interaction is never presented as a user's saved result.

All posts offer a useful method **before** mentioning the product. The closing action stays “add your car and save one next job.” These posts are for the founder's own social accounts; forum rules apply separately. In particular, **do not paste this AI-written material into r/Miata submissions**, which ban AI-generated text posts. [r/Miata rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/Miata/).

## S01 — Give the part a job

**First three seconds:** close shot of a handwritten “Cabin filter” line. Say “Before you buy the next part, give it a job.” Overlay: **Which job does it belong to?**

**Solo shot list:** 0–3s note; 3–10s write job + exact part reference; 10–17s draw separate estimate/actual fields; 17–end show the live public example's task and a simple CapCar closing frame. Keep the camera stationary.

**Exact spoken script:**

> Before you buy the next part, give it a job. Write down what you're trying to do, the exact part reference and your estimate. Leave actual spending blank until you've paid. That gives you a useful starting point next time you open the list. CapCar keeps the plan with your car. Add your car and save one next job at capcar.dev. An account saves it.

**Exact caption:**

> A useful parts list starts with the job. Keep the reference and estimate together, then record the actual cost when you know it. The CapCar screen here is an illustrative example. Start your own plan at capcar.dev; an account is needed to save. Free during beta.

**Takeaway before product:** connect a part to a task and separate estimate from actual.

**Authentic assets required:** founder-created note plus a current public homepage example capture. No purchased-part photo required. If using a real part, it must be the founder's own or used with permission.

**Reuse:** same clean master for TikTok and Shorts; text version is the caption. Link tag `utm_content=s01`. Verbal capcar.dev matters even where a caption URL is not clickable. Production target: 20 minutes shared with S02, plus simple subtitles in the shared export block.

**Exact caption:**

**Authentic assets:** own prop/note and actual public planned-task UI. Do not fake a delivery, shop purchase or installed modification.

**Reuse:** TikTok primary; clean export becomes a Reel or Short with “Bought ≠ fitted” retained. The caption also works as an Instagram Story note. `s02`. Production target: 20 minutes shared with S01; reuse only within reserved slots.

## Caption links and review

When an existing platform supports a usable profile link, use the real public homepage with consistent tags. Example:

```text
https://capcar.dev/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=one_car_one_plan_2026_10&utm_content=s12
```

Use `instagram`, `tiktok`, `youtube`, `threads` or `bluesky` for the actual placement, keeping the `sNN` asset ID across reuse. Do not put a creator name, personal information or private vehicle data into the URL. Where a link isn't usable, the spoken “capcar.dev” is sufficient. Do not claim that UTMs persist through account creation; [measurement limits](growth-plan.md) apply.

Before each publication: read it aloud; verify the visible UI still matches the claim; retain any example/preview labels and asset credits; inspect for private data; check there is a useful takeaway before the product mention. Adapt wording if the founder would not naturally say it. Do not turn a failed interaction into a polished success clip.

Record founder minutes, relevant replies and consented onboarding/first-useful-action signals weekly. Saves and view counts can help choose a topic, but they do not establish activation. Four text assets and eight videos are deliverable drafts; platform reuse is optional when replies or setup help deserve the time.
