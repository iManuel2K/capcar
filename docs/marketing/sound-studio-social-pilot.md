# CapCar social pilot: Which would you listen to twice?

**Draft for review.** The [live Sound Studio](https://capcar.dev/sound-studio)
has an A/B comparison section. The current catalog includes Honda F20C and
Volvo 850 T5 recordings. These are different cars and recording conditions,
so the post asks about preference. It makes no claim about measured loudness,
a specific exhaust upgrade, fitment, or how either would sound on the
founder's BMW. No live community trend or prior post performance was verified
for this draft.

Use the sequence **pick → listen → compare → reply**. It works with two full
phone captures and a simple cover.

| Slide | On-image copy | Visual to capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Which would you listen to twice?** | Founder-owned car photo or a close shot of headphones beside a phone. Small line: Honda F20C / Volvo 850 T5. |
| 2 | **Pick A and B** | Full Sound Studio screenshot: assign Honda F20C to A and Volvo 850 T5 to B. Keep the recording labels legible. |
| 3 | **Listen to both** | Full listening desk screenshot with both recordings and their play controls visible. Use matching phone frames for slides 2 and 3. |
| 4 | **Which one are you replaying?** | A / B labels at equal size, plus `capcar.dev/sound-studio`. Small note: different recordings and conditions. |

Use the ice-blue accent on charcoal. Keep the screenshots uncropped where
the controls or labels matter. If recording a Reel with the clips, verify
the current sources and attribution requirements first. The catalog credits
Honda F20C to Tyler Riddle (CC BY 3.0) and Volvo 850 T5 to Jonas Tittmann
(CC BY-SA 4.0). Keep the Reel silent or use your own voice if reuse cannot
be cleared. No recording is needed for the still carousel.

**LinkedIn draft**

> Two car sound clips can give you a preference. They can’t tell you which car is louder in real life.
>
> That’s why CapCar’s Sound Studio gives each recording its own A/B slot. You can switch between them without hunting for the second clip. The labels and sources stay with each recording, and the listening desk explains why different microphones and recording conditions matter.
>
> I’d start with Honda F20C and Volvo 850 T5. What do you listen for first: the pitch, the idle, or the way it climbs?
