# CapCar Concept Studio — 3D model credits

These 25 creator-made vehicle references are free to view in CapCar's public
Concept Studio and private garage. A manufacturer's name identifies the depicted
vehicle; it does not imply endorsement, an official manufacturer asset or verified
dimensions. Vehicle designs and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

## Sources and processing

Original creator, model and license metadata were checked against Sketchfab's
public model API on **18 October 2026**. The selected GLBs were redistributed by
[pedrosoandre/carros](https://github.com/pedrosoandre/carros) at revision
[`0a56de377f02f2574d781f6920fed08d6423ec2f`](https://github.com/pedrosoandre/carros/tree/0a56de377f02f2574d781f6920fed08d6423ec2f).
The upstream catalog metadata is [MIT-licensed](./UPSTREAM-LICENSE.txt); the individual assets retain their
own Creative Commons licenses listed below.

Upstream modified the original files for web delivery: vertex welding, geometry
simplification, removal of unused objects/materials/textures, and Meshopt
compression. These are compressed derivatives, not the original downloads.
CapCar presents them at a normalized viewing scale and may change the displayed
body color where a separate body-paint material is identified. The models are
oriented for consistent camera views; the E36 model's authored presentation floor
is omitted from the viewer so the vehicle itself defines its scale. Exact vertex
bounds are used for visual framing. The underlying downloadable assets retain
their geometry and original metadata. These models are
visual references and do not establish compatibility, dimensional accuracy or
factory specifications. Original baked finishes are retained when safe paint
materials have not been identified. No game-port or known game-ripped asset is
included in this selected collection.

CapCar also decodes Meshopt geometry offline and repacks buffers into standalone
GLBs so browsers do not download a separate geometry decoder. Large textures are
resized for mobile viewing: opaque color and emissive images are re-encoded as
JPEG; alpha-bearing images retain PNG transparency; large normal and packed
material maps are resized and remain lossless PNG. The per-file manifest records
the final texture dimensions, encodings and file sizes. These optimization and
display changes do not alter the original attribution or asset license.

The license for each of the 24 CC BY entries allows commercial use,
adaptation and redistribution with attribution, a license link and notice of
changes. The Porsche Carrera 4S model is **CC BY-SA 4.0**: adaptations of that
specific asset must also be distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. This applies to the
model asset, not to independent CapCar application code. Preserve these credits
and the corresponding license when redistributing a model.
