# Pricing

A single CapCar plan labelled Free (localized in all five languages). No numeric prices,
billing periods, paid tiers, checkout, invented quotas or promises of lifetime pricing.
Use the existing public typography, semantic colours, chrome and footer. A centred heading,
compact outlined plan, check-list features and one full-width garage CTA reflow on phones.
Keep the note about separate retailer purchases/connected AI service charges.

Reference requested by the user: https://21st.dev/@aymanch-03/components/pricing-section
by Ayman Echakar, listed as MIT on 21st. The public page's demo and preview were inspected.
The component's underlying source requires a signed-in 21st account; the available browser
was signed out. This is an independent CapCar implementation of that public pricing-card
layout, not an installation or copy of the gated component source. No registry dependency
or 21st authentication was added. Billing tabs and additional tiers are intentionally omitted.
