# CapCar social

Start with one useful idea an enthusiast can try. Make Instagram the visual
lead when the channel is unspecified. Adapt the idea to LinkedIn when the
founder's product decision or engineering trade-off is worth sharing.

## Inputs and evidence

- Read [the social calendar](../../../docs/marketing/social-calendar.md) and
  the relevant product source before asserting app behavior. Record whether
  each important claim was observed live, supported by code, or supplied by
  the founder. Do not present an untested interaction as a live result.
- Accept a topic, approved captures, a founder note, or a metrics export.
  Continue with what is available. Mark missing assets or evidence plainly.
  Use founder-owned car footage, approved full app screenshots, or clearly
  labelled public examples. Keep owner data, account screens, email, plates,
  VINs and exact private locations out of public drafts and visuals.
- Choose a single post goal: useful saves, shares, qualified replies, or a
  relevant visit. Choose one call to action that fits it.

## Nine steps

| Step | Do this | Return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Listen | Find recent public Reddit or community discussions tied to the topic. Check the dates, subreddit rules, and what owners actually asked. Use [customer research](../customer-research/SKILL.md) for a broader pass. No scraping gated data or posting replies. | Two or three linked questions or patterns. If unavailable, label founder or app evidence as the starting point. |
| 2. Pick a structure | Study the sequence of a relevant public example or the user's own comparable posts. Borrow the storytelling pattern, never the phrasing, design or creator assets. If performance isn't visible, describe it as a reference, not a winning format. | A short outline, such as question → method → proof → next step. |
| 3. Draft | Write from the original idea and verified facts. For LinkedIn, show a concrete trade-off the founder can stand behind. Use [social](../social/SKILL.md) for channel-specific format and reuse. | A post or caption that makes sense without the other channel. |
| 4. Open well | Write up to three hooks, then pick the one the clip or first slide can prove. Prefer a car detail, ordinary frustration or specific question to a broad claim. | The opening line or cover headline. |
| 5. Match the voice | Use the founder's approved examples when supplied. Write plainly, with a car enthusiast's curiosity and a family-friendly tone. Keep the product pitch brief. Do not invent personal anecdotes. | Natural wording grounded in the input. |
| 6. Edit | Use [humanizer](../humanizer/SKILL.md) to remove filler, canned contrasts and forced hooks. Keep the verified facts and caveats that matter. | Final copy ready to review. |
| 7. Show the method | Turn the takeaway into one useful visual: an A/B pair, checklist, timeline or full app screenshot. Use the existing CapCar palette, one accent and legible labels. If the user asks for a finished visual, render and check it; label a shot list as a plan. | One visual or a precise production brief. |
| 8. Build the carousel | Use [carousel frameworks](../social/references/carousel-frameworks.md). Start with the car or the question, use a full screenshot where the app needs to be understood, and keep one idea per slide. Use a 1080 × 1350 canvas for the CapCar portrait series. Check the finished export on a phone before approval. | Slide copy, capture instructions, caption and source/credit notes. |
| 9. Review results | Compare the same format and age since publication. Use Instagram saves/shares for useful content and LinkedIn impressions/relevant replies for discussion. Match metric denominators and separate what happened from why it might have happened. Use [analytics](../analytics/SKILL.md) for tagging. | A measured result and one next test, or a reporting plan if numbers are missing. |

For any public product claim, preserve the difference between illustrative
data, a real owner record and an AI suggestion. Avoid unsupported reach,
safety, legal, fitment, power, route or price claims. Credit assets and check
reuse rights before export; an in-app license does not automatically permit
reposting. Drafts and review only unless the user explicitly requests
publication. Do not connect accounts, schedule posts or contact anyone as
part of this workflow.
